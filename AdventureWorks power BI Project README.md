# AdventureWorks Business Performance Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyses AdventureWorks business data to identify insights across sales, customer performance, inventory, supply chain operations, and staffing.

## Business Objective
To understand business performance, identify inventory risks, and examine whether supply chain and staffing conditions could affect key revenue-generating products.

## Tools Used
- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Excel data sources

## Analysis Performed
- Sales and revenue analysis
- Customer and territory performance
- Product performance
- Inventory and vendor analysis
- HR and staffing analysis

## Key Insights
- Total revenue reached approximately $109.85 million.
- Seven products were below their reorder points.
- None of the top 10 revenue-generating products were among those seven products.
- Order fulfilment rate was approximately 99.1%.

## Recommendations
Monitor products below their reorder points, review inventory replenishment policies, and continue tracking supplier performance.
